Independent Bionic Hand Project

Designer & Developer — Independent 12th grade — ongoing

Overview

An independent, low-cost prosthetic hand built around Arduino, 3D-printed structural components, and a mix of servo actuation with flex and voice sensor input — aimed at making assistive tech more affordable and accessible.

Prototype photoassets/bionic-hand/prototype.jpg
CAD renderassets/bionic-hand/cad.jpg

Technical Approach

Flex sensors and a voice-command input translate into servo-driven finger actuation, with ongoing work on cleaning up noisy sensor readings and improving grip consistency across different signal conditions.
