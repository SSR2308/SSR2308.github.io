Overview

A beach-safety app that turns messy, scattered coastal data — tide, swell, wind, and water-quality feeds — into a single plain-language risk score, so lifeguards and beachgoers can answer "is it safe today?" faster than reading four different data sources.

Features

  • Real-time risk scoring from tide, swell, wind, and water-quality feeds, with plain-language reasons (e.g. "strong side-current, low visibility, lightning window").
  • Hazard map for lifeguards and regulars to pin rip channels, jellyfish, and closed access points.
  • TideBot — a retrieval-based AI assistant that answers beach-condition questions using NOAA advisories and local postings, not general chat.
  • Fishing guides built on the same underlying condition data.

Process

Built and treated like a real system rather than a prototype: logged conditions against actual lifeguard calls and personal saves, tuned risk thresholds against that data, and rewrote edge cases as the ocean produced conditions the model hadn't seen. Tested with lifeguards and beach regulars before launch.
